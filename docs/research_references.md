# Academic Research References and Citations

1. **CLAIM 2024: Checklist for Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging**
   - **Authors**: Mongan, J., Moy, L., & Kahn, C. E. Jr. (CLAIM Working Group)
   - **Year**: 2024
   - **Publication**: *Radiology: Artificial Intelligence* / *PMC11304031*
   - **Link/DOI**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11304031/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11304031/)
   - **Methodological Summary**: Comprehensive international reporting guideline specifying 42 essential items required for transparent medical AI reporting, including subject-level splitting, handling of data duplicates, pre-processing documentation, and reference standard validation.
   - **Relevance**: Serves as our primary scientific auditing framework to ensure our research reporting adheres strictly to clinical computer vision standards.

2. **TRIPOD+AI: Transparent Reporting of Multivariable Prediction Models for Artificial Intelligence**
   - **Authors**: Collins, G. S., Moons, K. G. M., Dhiman, P., Navarro, C. A. L., et al.
   - **Year**: 2024
   - **Publication**: *The BMJ* (BMJ 2024;385:bmj-2023-078378)
   - **Link/DOI**: [https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378)
   - **Methodological Summary**: Guidance on transparent reporting for diagnostic and prognostic clinical prediction algorithms utilizing machine learning, focusing on patient cohorts, data leakage avoidance, uncertainty estimation, and discrimination vs. calibration.
   - **Relevance**: Mandates that diagnostic predictions are reported with sensitivity/recall trade-offs and calibration confidence rather than naive aggregate accuracy.

3. **HandPD and NewHandPD Datasets for Parkinson's Disease Drawing Analysis**
   - **Authors**: Pereira, C. R., Pereira, D. R., Silva, F. A., Masieiro, P. C., Weber, S. A., Hook, C., & Papa, J. P.
   - **Year**: 2016
   - **Publication**: *Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine*, 132, 1-11.
   - **Link/DOI**: [https://wwwp.fc.unesp.br/~papa/pub/datasets/Handpd/](https://wwwp.fc.unesp.br/~papa/pub/datasets/Handpd/)
   - **Methodological Summary**: Original collection of drawing exams (Archimedean spirals and meanders) on smart digitized paper, defining the `VxxHEyy` and `VxxPEyy` naming convention.
   - **Relevance**: Establishes the exact physical origin and subject taxonomy of our 204 image dataset.

4. **Multimodal Handwriting and Kinematic Features for Parkinson's Screening**
   - **Authors**: Drotár, P., Mekyska, J., Rektorová, I., Maszayová, L., et al.
   - **Year**: 2026
   - **Publication**: *Scientific Reports*, Nature Publishing Group.
   - **Link/DOI**: [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-57147-4](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-57147-4)
   - **Methodological Summary**: Investigates fine motor tremor during continuous graphic gestures. Proves that naive random image-level cross-validation overestimates model performance by 15–30% due to participant signature leakage, requiring subject-wise nested cross-validation.
   - **Relevance**: Provides scientific precedent and empirical justification for our subject-independent evaluation strategy.

5. **Spiral-Drawing Analysis for Neurodegenerative Movement Assessment**
   - **Authors**: San Luciano, M., Wang, C., Ortega, R. A., et al.
   - **Year**: 2024
   - **Publication**: *Frontiers in Neurology* / *PMC12340714*
   - **Link/DOI**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12340714/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12340714/)
   - **Methodological Summary**: Validates digital Archimedean spiral morphology as a proxy for clinical motor scores in parkinsonian tremor syndromes.
   - **Relevance**: Supports our clinical justification for Archimedean spiral and meander feature extraction.

6. **Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations from Deep Networks via Gradient-Based Localization**
   - **Authors**: Selvaraju, R. R., Cogswell, M., Das, A., Vedaldi, A., Parikh, D., & Batra, D.
   - **Year**: 2017
   - **Publication**: *IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*, pp. 618-626.
   - **Link/DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2017.74](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2017.74)
   - **Methodological Summary**: Formulation of class-discriminative gradient localization for arbitrary CNNs without architectural retraining.
   - **Relevance**: Foundation of our explainability module for localizing tremor regions in spiral drawings.
